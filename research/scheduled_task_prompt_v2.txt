EVIDENCE TIERS — tag every radar score
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A company's own homepage is not proof of a claim like "we're the #1 GEO provider". Every score rests on a source, and the source's tier caps what it can justify:

- IND (Independent) — third-party news, funding databases, B2B review directories (Clutch, GoodFirms, G2, Capterra), VC portfolios, or a reproducible live citation test. Highest weight.
- ART (Verifiable artifact) — an objective fact anyone can check on the site itself: sitemap page counts, an llms.txt file, RTL markup, a published price. Proves the fact, not the superlative.
- SELF (Self-reported) — the company's own marketing claim, a ranking it publishes about itself, an unverified case study. Weak: capped low, and NEVER the sole basis for a market-authority or proven-outcomes score.

Per-axis admissible evidence:
- Axis 1 Arabic citation share: live prompt basket only (IND).
- Axis 2 content depth, 4 technical GEO, 5 adoption access: ART.
- Axis 3 feature breadth: ART, cross-checked against independent rankings where they name the company.
- Axis 6 market authority: IND ONLY — funding, VC backing, third-party directory presence. Self-published rankings are excluded entirely.
- Axis 7 proven outcomes: a named case study with a third-party-checkable metric. A self-published case study is SELF and capped at 6.

Record tier + source per axis compactly in the radarEvidence cell, e.g. "1:IND live basket 18 Aug; 2:ART sitemap 98/49; 6:IND Fortune Series C".
